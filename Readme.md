# Vakilai

VakilAI is an AI-driven legal intelligence platform designed to bridge the gap between complex civic bureaucracy and ordinary citizens. It translates obscure legal statutes, confusing public portals, and fragmented government notices into actionable, plain-language legal guidance and ready-to-file applications.

Live Deployment: https://oosc-neural-nexus.vercel.app/
> **Important Notice Regarding First Load (Render Cold Start):** 
> The backend and AI microservice are deployed on free-tier Render instances. If the server has been idle, the initial request may take **45–60 seconds** to wake up from hibernation. If you encounter a temporary server delay or timeout on your first prompt, please give it a minute and start a new chat.
 # Problem & Challenge Statement
 # The Problem 
 Millions of citizens hold legitimate rights and entitlements ranging from consumer guarantees and tenant protections to Right to Information (RTI) access and social welfare benefits. However, these rights remain underutilized because:Legal, governmental, and bureaucratic phrasing is intimidating, dense, and opaque.Official rules are fragmented across unsearchable PDFs, obscure state portals, and disjointed circulars.There is no unified conversational interface that extracts a user's personal context and translates it into direct, actionable steps.
 # The Challenge
 Build an AI system that empowers citizens to understand and act on their civic and legal rights by transforming bureaucratic friction into a clear, guided, and automated workflow.
 Core Features
 # Rights Navigator
 Breaks down tenancy disputes, consumer grievances, and workplace issues into plain English/Hindi, providing rights summaries, checklists, and local escalation channels (e.g., DLSA, Rent Tribunals).
 # RTI Drafting Agent
 Converts plain-language citizen queries into legally formatted Right to Information applications targeted to the correct Public Information Officer (PIO) and department.
 # Scheme Eligibility Advisor
 Evaluates a user's profile (age, occupation, income, caste/category, landholding) to identify eligible Central and State welfare programs and scholarships.
 # Conversational Case Assistant
 An interactive chat workflow that guides the citizen, asks only for missing essential information, and extracts structured legal case summaries.
 # Multi-Language Support
 Delivers legal empowerment across regional languages to break linguistic and literacy barriers.
